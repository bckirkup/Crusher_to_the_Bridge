# Tranche 58 — event-moment emesis co-exposure is partially licensed, but its magnitudes are not

**Register rows fed / supersession.** Runs the literature-readiness half of
[`../ledger/NORO-EMESIS-BOOTSTRAP-01.md`](../ledger/NORO-EMESIS-BOOTSTRAP-01.md)
§6 lever 1 before any emesis co-exposure design. It **supersedes nothing**,
**withdraws no measurement**, moves no constant, and does not implement or
specify engine code. Marks 2000 remains a post-repair check only; nothing here
is selected against VSP, Park, attack-rate, passenger/crew, posting-frequency,
Diamond Princess, or any other anchor.

**Status:** evidence assembled and interpreted. **Design-readiness verdict:
partially licensed.** The literature licenses a *coarse discrete-event spatial
and temporal structure* — a local splash/deposition footprint plus a
minutes-to-hours aerosol persistence tail — but it does **not** license the
event-moment concentration, near-field receiving volume, inhalation fraction,
decay rate, or distance kernel needed to make that structure quantitative.

**Source discipline.** Consensus MCP was available. Every search used
`include_full_text_chunks: true`, named the quantity and unit, and used no
filters. Full text was opened through Consensus for the primary rows except
where marked otherwise. Origins use the register vocabulary: **R** Results,
**Tn** table *n*, **Fn** figure *n*, **Me** Methods, **Ab** abstract only,
**Sec** secondary/review, **Tr** transcribed. A blocked read is recorded as a
retrieval state, never as a null result. Grades are Crusher grades: **A** this
quantity in this setting, **B** analogous setting or surrogate, **C** inferred
or declared. No dose figure in this tranche rescues the open ledger's standing
caveat: every repository dose figure remains void pending refit.

## Result in one line

A real vomiting incident is **not** evidenced as a whole-zone, whole-epoch
well-mixed emission: measured surrogate droplets reach **1–3 m**, viable
surrogate virus is recovered in those far droplets, and aerosolized norovirus
RNA or surrogate infectivity can persist for **hours**. But the exact quantity
the design needs — a positive quantitative time-resolved post-emesis
concentration in the breathing zone of co-present people during the first
seconds-to-minutes — is **∅ null**,
so the only licensed implementation direction is a declared, swept, coarsely
bounded event-local pulse/footprint structure, not a fitted radius, rate, or
magnitude. Booth's all-negative viable-surrogate air samples are a measured
result, but not a quantitative concentration curve.

### 1.2 What is not measured — the null that matters

No retrieved study reports a **positive, quantitative, time-resolved airborne
norovirus concentration series after an actual or realistic vomiting event**
in a normally sized, ventilated occupied room. Alsved is a real-world
measurement but each sample is a 10-minute integral and the time coordinate is
a categorical recall of the last vomiting episode. Booth is a direct
post-vomiting air measurement in two 30-minute bins, but every viable-surrogate
sample was negative and the paper itself treats false negatives as plausible;
it therefore supplies no quantitative concentration or decay slope. Tung-
Thompson is a 15-minute integrated recovery fraction in a small chamber.
Rupprom and Meister measure generated-aerosol persistence, not emesis. These
measurements are mutually consistent with a minutes-to-hours tail, but they do
not jointly identify emission mass, receiving volume, mixing, settling,
inactivation, or sampler recovery.

**Null statement:** for a positive, quantitative
`post_vomiting_airborne_concentration_copies_per_m3` series as a function of
minutes since event, distance, and height, the literature is **∅ null** after
quantity-and-unit searches. This is not a claim that the concentration is zero,
and it does not erase Booth's measured non-detection.

### 2.3 Inferred or calculated, not measured

Tung-Thompson's discussion calculates that a **100 µm** droplet of density
**1.41 g/cm³** has a predicted settling velocity of **0.46 m/s**, so it would
fall **1 m** in a few seconds, and that a bare **32 nm** norovirus particle
would have a Stokes settling velocity of **4.7×10⁻⁸ m/s**. These are physical
order-of-magnitude calculations, not measurements of vomitus droplets; grade
**C**, origin **Sec/Discussion**. They motivate separating large ballistic
droplets from small persistent aerosol particles but cannot set either
fraction or radius.

### 2.4 What is not measured

No retrieved study measures the **particle-size distribution emitted by an
actual vomiting event** at the mouth or breathing zone, nor a spatially
resolved deposition-density map in copies or infectious units per unit area.
Booth measures where fluid and surrogate virus can land, not how much human
norovirus lands on tables, chairs, clothing, mucous membranes, or food in a
real room.

### 3.1 Measured epidemiological associations — exposure happened, route unresolved

|---|---:|---|---|---|

| Kirking et al., *Clinical Infectious Diseases* 2010, DOI 10.1086/651597 | after a flight with multiple ill tour-group members, illness among non-tour passengers was associated with sitting near any tour-group member, adjusted relative risk **7.5** (**95% CI 1.7–33.6**), and aisle seating, adjusted relative risk **11.0** (**95% CI 1.4–84.9**) | short-duration co-transport exposure is measured, but there were multiple ill passengers and no single emesis source event; aerosol, direct contact, and shared surfaces are inseparable. | Evans 2002 concert hall | same-level seating the following day: relative risk **7.1** (**95% CI 5.4–9.2**) | most cases were not present during the incident, so this is evidence for persistent environmental/fomite exposure rather than the pre-cleanup co-present pulse | **B** | **R** |

### 3.2 Hypothesis / model output — Marks is not uniquely airborne

Xiao, Tang & Li 2017, DOI 10.3390/ijerph14121571, re-analysed Marks 2000 with
a multi-agent fomite model. Under assumed waiter serving pathways and surface
behaviours, a fomite route could reproduce a table-level infection-risk
pattern similar to the reported attack-rate gradient. This is **model output,
not measurement** and adopts no value here. Its evidentiary force is narrow
but important: the Marks gradient cannot by itself discriminate an airborne
event pulse from waiter-mediated surface transmission, especially because the
original incident was non-projectile and rapidly cleaned.

### 3.3 Null

No retrieved outbreak or laboratory study measures the exposure or infection
risk of co-present people **only during the seconds-to-minutes before
evacuation, avoidance, or cleanup**, nor does any study measure the occupancy
decay, avoidance radius, or cleanup onset after vomiting in a dining room.
Attack rates are post-incident endpoints integrated over the rest of the meal,
classroom day, flight, or venue use. They cannot be inverted into an
event-moment dose without adding exactly the mechanism assumptions under test.

## 4. Does the evidence license a discrete-event co-exposure structure beyond whole-zone/whole-epoch dilution?

**Verdict: partially licensed.**

### 4.1 Licensed structure

1. **Discrete event, not continuous emission.** Alsved's within-3-h association,
   the aerosol-persistence measurements, and the vomiting-outbreak records all
   support conditioning the source on an emesis event. This was already the
   licensed mode; the new tranche does not reopen it.
2. **A local spatial footprint, not instant whole-zone uniformity.** Booth's
   fluorescent and viable-surrogate experiments measure droplet/splash reach
   to **1–3 m** and a strongly nonuniform deposition field. Evans and the
   outbreak spatial patterns independently show that exposure risk is spatially
   concentrated. A structure that instantly spreads all event mass uniformly
   over a large venue is therefore the wrong form.
3. **Two population classes at the event moment.** The coarse structure the
   evidence supports is an event-local splash/near-air footprint as an
   additional exposure class, not a boundary at which airborne exposure is
   known to begin. People and surfaces inside the footprint can receive direct
   deposition plus whatever airborne concentration exists there; people outside
   it can only be assigned room-air and environmental exposure with timing
   unspecified. The measured record does not support a third, finely resolved
   distance ring or a clock for the onset of far-field airborne exposure.
4. **A minutes-to-hours aerosol tail exists.** Human GII RNA persisted in
   generated aerosols for hours in Rupprom, and MNV infectivity persisted for
   hours in Meister. Purhonen is not counted toward persistence because it
   measured infectious surrogate delivery during continuing nebulizer exposure,
   not survival after the source stopped. Booth's non-detection of viable FCV
   in air does not negate the persistence rows because it is a surrogate,
   sampler- and assay-limited non-detection. The tail's existence is licensed;
   its rate is not.

### 4.2 Declared intervals/envelopes the evidence can carry — not adopted constants

These are bounds for a future design discussion only. They are **not** constant
updates and are not a license to implement them as fixed values.

| design quantity | evidence-carried envelope | grade / origin | limitation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| bulk-vomitus landing scale | **1.2 m × 1.6 m** main bulk in Booth 2014 | **B**, **R** | one simulator, water simulant, sealed empty chamber, three visual repeats |
| outer splash reach | **1–3 m** for viable-surrogate-bearing small droplets; wall splashes **>3 m longitudinal** and **>2.2 m lateral** in Booth 2014; abstract envelope **2.6 m lateral / ≥7.8 m²** | **B**, **R**/**T1**/**Ab** | upper-extent footprint, not a mean radius; no furniture, ventilation, or human movement |
| aerosol-persistence existence window | detectable generated human GII RNA to **2–4 h**; detectable infectious MNV to **9 h**, with **2.33 log₁₀** loss by **3 h** | **B**, **R** for Rupprom; **B**, **Ab** for Meister | generated aerosols, chamber systems, surrogate/instrument recovery; not post-emesis decay rates |
| real-world airborne RNA concentration envelope | **5–215 copies/m³** mean **31 copies/m³** in Alsved; **1.35×10¹–2.35×10³ genomes/m³** in Bonifait | **B**, **R** | healthcare background during outbreaks, not first-minute concentration after a vomiting event |
| particle-size envelope for small carriers | positive real-air fractions **<0.95 µm** and **>4.51 µm**; generated surrogate MMD around **0.89–1.1 µm**, Meister MMAD **2.38 µm** | **B**, **R**/**Ab** | size depends on generator, matrix and drying; no emesis-generated distribution exists |

### 4.3 Refused structure and magnitudes

The evidence does **not** license:

- a continuous distance kernel or a fitted exponential radius around the
  vomiting host;
- a point value for event-moment breathing-zone concentration;
- a point value for near-field effective volume or near/far exchange rate;
- a settling half-time, inactivation half-life, or airborne-decay constant for
  vomitus-derived aerosol;
- direct transfer of continuous-respiratory near-field magnitudes from Keil,
  Li, Parhizkar, or tranche 36 to episodic emesis;
- use of Marks 2000's attack-rate gradient as a parameter source;
- use of the Booth splash footprint as a hard binary radius in a furnished,
  ventilated dining room;
- inference that non-detection of viable FCV in Booth's air samples means no
  infectious aerosol exists.

### 4.4 Smallest measurement that would close the remaining null

The smallest decisive measurement is a realistic emesis-event chamber or
room experiment that simultaneously records: (i) time-resolved particle number
and size distribution at breathing height at several distances for **0–60 min**;
(ii) time-resolved human norovirus GII RNA and a cultivable surrogate in air;
(iii) spatially resolved deposition density on floor, table, chair and vertical
surfaces; and (iv) the room's ventilation/mixing rate. The critical design
feature is co-located time, distance, size, matrix, and volume measurement in
one experiment. Without that joint measurement, aerosol concentration remains
non-identifiable for the same reason tranche 28 recorded: emission mass,
aerosol fraction, receiving volume, and removal enter as a product.

### 4.5 One-paragraph consequence for the floor-loss boundary

Booth's footprint evidence means the current all-or-nothing floor-loss
boundary is structurally suspect: droplets that miss the declared high-touch
footprint are not all irrelevant floor loss, because small droplets can reach
at least **1–3 m** and carry viable surrogate virus. But this tranche does not
license a table/chair/service-item destination split, because no study measures
deposition density by receiving surface class in a furnished room. The only
licensed consequence is that a future design should carry an event-local
contamination footprint object rather than discard all non-touchable mass at
the zone boundary; the dimensions and surface allocation of that footprint
remain declared and swept.

## 6. Bottom line for the next implementation question

**Partially licensed.** The next implementation question is: can an emesis
event represent a declared event-local exposure class — same table/unit and a
coarse adjacent footprint — with the residual aerosol mass entering the room
pool over a swept minutes-to-hours time structure, while explicitly treating
its footprint dimensions and pulse duration as swept axes rather than evidence
constants?
