# Consensus tranche 53 — the norovirus share of fleet acute gastroenteritis

> **Status:** Reference.

Evidence retrieved 2026-10-22 for the maintainer's identified gap: *what
credible fraction of acute gastroenteritis (AGE) in the fleet is likely
norovirus*. Retrieval: one Consensus Pro research thread
([thread](https://consensus.app/search/research-question-what-fraction-of-acute-gastroent/RLuL_KqCSoKlMVGpeAj70w/))
plus two targeted `search` passes with `include_full_text_chunks`, one naming
outbreak-level etiology and one naming the all-cases prevalence. Origins are
marked per row (`R` Results, `Tn` Table n, `Ab` Abstract only, `Me` Methods,
`Sec` secondary, `Tr` transcribed).

## 1. The answer is denominator-dependent — three separable quantities

| Denominator | Norovirus share | Evidence class |
|---|---|---|
| Etiology-determined AGE **outbreaks** | **~0.80–0.97** | Measured, multiple surveillance periods |
| AGE **cases within outbreaks** | **~0.68–0.70** | Measured (VSP 2008–2014) |
| **All** AGE cases (outbreak + sporadic) | **unmeasured aboard ship**; floor ~0.10 passenger / ~0.03 crew, plausible ~0.2–0.3 | Floor measured; the rest inferred |

The headline "~90%+" figure answers the *outbreak* question. No study has
systematically tested the etiology of routine, non-outbreak shipboard AGE
presentations — that is a genuine measurement gap for every pathogen, not a
null on norovirus.

## 2. Outbreak-level — measured

- **Freeland et al. 2016**, *MMWR* 65(1), DOI 10.15585/mmwr.mm6501a1 (`R`,
  abstract + figure caption via chunks). CDC Vessel Sanitation Program,
  2008–2014, 29,107 voyages: 133 AGE outbreaks reported; 95 (71%) had clinical
  specimens tested; **92 of 95 were norovirus (96.8%** of tested outbreaks —
  the paper prints both 97% and "92%"; the count is 92/95). All 87
  viral-only outbreaks were norovirus, plus 4 mixed viral-bacterial and 1
  mixed viral-parasitic. Non-norovirus etiologies in the period:
  enterotoxigenic *E. coli* (6 outbreaks), *Shigella sonnei* (1),
  *Campylobacter jejuni* (1), *Clostridium perfringens* (1, crew-only),
  *Cyclospora cayetanensis* (1). As a share of all 133 reported outbreaks
  (including untested), norovirus is 69.2%.
- **Preston et al. 2026**, *Open Forum Infectious Diseases* 13, DOI
  10.1093/ofid/ofaf695.2359 (`Ab`, conference abstract, CDC data): **since
  2019, 81% of cruise-ship AGE outbreaks posted to the VSP website were
  attributed to norovirus**; GII.17 has become the predominant genotype over
  GII.4 in 2020–2025.
- **Cramer et al. 2003**, *MMWR* 51(49):1112–5, DOI 10.1001/jama.289.2.167
  (`Ab`): 2002 season — 21 outbreaks reported, **9 confirmed norovirus**, 3
  bacterial, 9 unknown etiology → 75% of the 12 etiology-determined outbreaks,
  43% of all reported. 2001: 4 of 7 outbreaks norovirus.
- **Cramer et al. 2004**, *J Infect Dis* 190(1), DOI 10.1086/420888 (`Ab`,
  `Me`): of 14 laboratory-confirmed cruise outbreaks in 2002, 12 (85.7%) were
  calicivirus, of which 11 were norovirus → **78.6% of confirmed outbreaks**.
- **Scaioli et al. 2013**, *Eur J Public Health* 23, DOI
  10.1093/eurpub/ckt123.192 (`Sec`, abstract citing CDC): "over 90% of
  diarrheal disease outbreaks on cruise ships are caused by norovirus".
- **Mouchtouri et al. 2024**, *Eurosurveillance* 29(10), DOI
  10.2807/1560-7917.es.2024.29.10.2300345 (`Ab`): norovirus has been the
  **leading cause of AGE outbreaks on cruise ships since 2006**.

**Synthesis:** across four surveillance eras the norovirus share of
etiology-determined cruise outbreaks sits in **~0.75–0.97**; the recent-era
point is ~0.81–0.97. The credible interval for "an outbreak posted to VSP is
norovirus" is **~0.8–0.95**.

## 3. Case-level aboard ship — partly measured

- **Freeland et al. 2016** (`R`): 129,678 passenger AGE cases in 2008–2014,
  of which only **19,273 (14.9%) were part of an outbreak**; of those
  outbreak-associated cases, **13,568 (70.4%) were in laboratory-confirmed
  norovirus outbreaks**. Crew: 43,132 AGE cases, 1,984 (4.6%)
  outbreak-associated, of which 1,343 (67.7%) in confirmed norovirus
  outbreaks.
  → **Measured floor:** norovirus outbreak cases are ≥10.5% of *all*
  passenger AGE cases and ≥3.1% of all crew AGE cases. The etiology of the
  remaining ~85–95% (non-outbreak presentations) was never tested.
- **Wikswo et al. 2011**, *Clin Infect Dis* 52(9):1116–22, DOI
  10.1093/cid/cir144 (`Ab`): within one high-morbidity 2009 outbreak,
  **12/14 (85.7%) tested specimens were norovirus-positive**; 15.4% of
  surveyed passengers met the AGE case definition and **40% of them never
  reported to the infirmary** — infirmary surveillance detected only 60% of
  AGE cases. Relevant to any "fleet AGE" denominator built on reported cases.
- **De Bellis et al. 2025**, *J Travel Med* 32(7), DOI 10.1093/jtm/taaf059
  (`R`): 121 GI cases over a 7-day Mediterranean voyage (9.7% passenger
  cumulative incidence); stools from 96% of cases; **PCR-confirmed norovirus
  in 65/121 (53.7%)** — even inside a declared norovirus outbreak, case-level
  confirmation is partial.
- **Chimonas et al. 2008**, *J Travel Med* 15(3):177–83, DOI
  10.1111/j.1708-8305.2008.00200.x (`Ab`): 4/7 clinical specimens
  norovirus-positive in a 359-case outbreak.
- **Mouchtouri et al. 2024** (`R`, Table 1 + `Ab`): pooled norovirus attack
  rate across 45 outbreaks (1990–2020) — **passengers 7% (95% CI 5–9)**, crew
  **2% (95% CI 0–3)**; person-to-person the most frequent transmission mode
  (35/45 outbreaks).

## 4. Shoreside baseline — measured (for the sporadic/non-outbreak share)

- **Ahmed et al. 2014**, *Lancet Infect Dis* 14(8), DOI
  10.1016/s1473-3099(14)70767-4 (`Ab` + `R`): pooled norovirus prevalence in
  **187,336 AGE cases = 18% (95% CI 17–20)**; community 24%, outpatient 20%,
  inpatient 17%; developed countries 20%.
- **Liao et al. 2021**, *Microb Pathog* 160, DOI
  10.1016/j.micpath.2021.105259 (`Ab`): 2014–2021 update, 842,926 cases —
  **16% (95% CI 15–17)**; gradient community 20% → outpatients 18% → hospital
  17% → inpatients 15%.
- **Zhang et al. 2024**, *Front Public Health* 12, DOI
  10.3389/fpubh.2024.1373322 (`Ab` + `R`): post-GII.4-Sydney era (2012–2022),
  85,798 sporadic cases — **19.0% (95% CI 16.7–21.4)**; developed countries
  16.25%.
- **Li et al. 2023**, *Viruses* 15(6), DOI 10.3390/v15061336 (`Ab`): China
  2009–2021, 991,786 acute-diarrhea patients — **11.54% positive**.
- **Farfán-García et al. 2020**, *PLoS Negl Trop Dis* 14(8), DOI
  10.1371/journal.pntd.0008375 (`R`, Table 5): case-control *attributable
  fraction* — norovirus GII explains **14% (95% CI 10–18)** of
  moderate-to-severe pediatric AGE; the largest single-pathogen share.

**Synthesis:** shoreside, norovirus accounts for **~15–20% of sporadic AGE
cases** in developed-country settings — an order below its shipboard
outbreak share.

## 5. What the fleet's "credible fraction" then is

- **Outbreaks:** ~0.8–0.95 (§2, measured).
- **Outbreak-associated cases:** ~0.7 (§3, measured).
- **All AGE cases:** measured floor ~0.10 (passengers) from outbreak
  attribution alone. If non-outbreak shipboard AGE carries the shoreside
  sporadic share (~0.15–0.20, §4), the total lands at roughly
  **~0.24–0.28 for passengers** (0.105 + 0.85 × [0.15–0.20]) and **~0.18–0.22
  for crew**. This compositing is *inference* — shipboard sporadic etiology
  is untested — so the honest declared interval is **~0.10–0.30**, and it is
  state-dependent: it rides up sharply while an outbreak is running and back
  toward the sporadic share between them.

## 6. Not licensed / warnings

- The ~0.9 outbreak share is **not** a case fraction; quoting it as "the
  norovirus share of fleet AGE" conflates denominators.
- The all-cases composite is an inference bound, not a measurement — no
  shipboard non-outbreak etiology series exists for *any* pathogen; record
  as a measured gap, not a null on norovirus.
- Within-outbreak PCR confirmation runs ~0.54–0.86 (§3), so even attributing
  every case of a confirmed outbreak to norovirus over-assigns by ~1.2–1.9×.
- De Bellis 2025 DOI verified against the publisher (the thread's prose
  printed a stale medRxiv-era identifier; JTM vo. 32(7) taaf059 is the
  version of record).
