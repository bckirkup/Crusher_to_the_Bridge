| [pathogen_class_structure_decision.md](pathogen_class_structure_decision.md) | **All of it** — a decision record, not a mechanism spec; it authorises no fit and adopts no dose value | The pathogen-side counterpart of [fleet_emergence_decision.md](fleet_emergence_decision.md): strain heterogeneity in the dose-response is structured as a small number of **classes**, GII.4 versus non-GII.4, and neither a per-genotype table (unidentifiable — the VSP series carries no genotype in any of its 428 postings) nor a single scalar (refuted by ~2.6 logs of spread). It rules that the variability is **between-voyage, not between-agent**, since a voyage is seeded by one importation, and notes that a per-founder dose multiplier *m* already divides that strain's ID50 by exactly *m* — a literal β reparameterisation for the closed form, an ID50 shift without one for the exact confluent-hypergeometric path — so no new dose-response field is needed. Prohibits a genotype-indexed dose table, fitting class shares to VSP, and widening β to stand in for strain spread. Its §4 records three in-tree findings: founders are registered with all four phenotype offsets at 1.0, so genotype is a label with no physics; `PhenotypeEffectRanges.transmissibility` is ±0.1 log against a 2.6-log cloud, so mutation aboard cannot generate it; and the uniform 1/3 `prior_genotype_distribution` becomes load-bearing with no typing provenance behind it |
