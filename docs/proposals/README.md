> behaviour.**

A document lives here until the artifact it specifies exists in-tree; then it
moves to its subject directory or to the `docs/` root. Do not cite these as
documentation of what the model does.

Read it before implementing R2–R5; this proposal changes no register value, grade, interval or count |
| [surface_decay_biphasic_spec.md](surface_decay_biphasic_spec.md) | **All of it, deliberately.** No `k_wet`, no `k_dry`, no `t_dry` — §7.1 refuses to supply them, because adopting a biphasic form with fitted rates would trade one unfounded scalar for three | Influenza surface decay is biphasic in **time since deposition**, and the phase boundary is drying: the hazard falls 2.7× between 0–4 h and 4–9 h inside one Greatorex 2011 run on one coupon, and the cross-study rate orders on observation window (ρ = −0.67) with matrix held fixed. It re-scopes #60 from Weibull-versus-exponential to **biphasic-by-drying versus exponential, per arm rather than once for both** — the window artifact is present on influenza and absent on norovirus — and corrects [`field_repair_sequence.md`](field_repair_sequence.md) §1 on which axis the influenza row is curve-shaped: the environmental covariates R2 was scoped around largely collapse on an HVAC-pinned ship, and the live axis is time. #36 still runs first, and this document sharpens what it must answer |
| [exterior_zone_environment_spec.md](exterior_zone_environment_spec.md) | **All of it, deliberately.** No exposure class, no aerosol dilution factor, no UV or solar inactivation constant, no AHU membership change, no occupancy change, and none of its §3(a)/(b)/(c) positions selected | Exterior zones already exist and describe *themselves* as "semi-open", "open-air" and "outdoor recreation deck" on every cruise platform, while no zone record on any platform carries a humidity, temperature, insolation or exposure field — the only outdoor-air concept in the engine is `BALCONY_AEROSOL_REDUCTION`, keyed on `cabin_ventilation_type` and applied to cabin corridors. The load-bearing finding is that the current treatment is **sign-reversed rather than neutral**: exterior zones are members of recirculating AHU networks at `oa_fraction: 0.2`, so mega's `Main_Pool_Deck`, `Waterpark` and `Sports_Court` share `AHU_Network_Aft_Dining_Rec` at 10 ACH with `Windjammer` and `Kids_Club` — an open pool deck is a duct-coupled aerosol *source* into the indoor buffet where physics expects a sink. Path A agrees rather than correcting it: `platform.prj` puts every zone at 293.15 K with no ambient path, and the ContamX compare job injects its tracer at the open-air `CentralPark`. `_surface_survival(profile)` has no zone argument, so a sun-exposed, salt-wetted handrail decays as a cabin doorknob does, and the `Free`-zone leisure draw is uniform and capacity-blind. It qualifies [`surface_decay_biphasic_spec.md`](surface_decay_biphasic_spec.md) §5, whose HVAC-pinned argument holds for the interior and fails for these zones |
