Cabin/public gradient: 4.02x
Park observed gradient: roughly 100-300x

Shedder-hour asymmetry required for a 100x gradient, holding the implemented constants fixed:
  needed shedder-hours(cabin)/shedder-hours(public) = 75
  A cabin cannot accumulate that many more shedder-hours than a public zone;
  the gradient is therefore not reachable by hand transfer at any occupancy.

Interpretation: the hand-transfer chain reproduces public-space contamination,
and cannot reproduce sick-cabin contamination. Park's cabin figures are the
signature of direct emesis/faecal deposition in a small bathroom, which is a
deposition mechanism the model does not have.
