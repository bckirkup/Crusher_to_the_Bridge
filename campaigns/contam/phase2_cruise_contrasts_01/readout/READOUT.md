- Phase 2 is a tracer-only four-cruise contrast panel: 1e6 tracer units per seed, `natural_decay_rate=0`, baseline `filter_efficiency=0.5`, 12 one-hour epochs, seeds 1-20, four cruise hulls, and the frozen seven-arm grammar (`baseline`, `oa_050`, `filter_090`, `oa_050_filter_090`, `eq_gain_025_filter`, `ach_150_supply`, `oa_080`).

- P is a bounded committed-PRJ baseline for the four hulls, `baseline` arm only; it cannot represent JSON-only arm mutations.
- No agents, pathogens, dose-response, epidemiological constants, VSP/Park/attack-rate anchors, Ship X, or Edison data-return packaging are part of this readout.
