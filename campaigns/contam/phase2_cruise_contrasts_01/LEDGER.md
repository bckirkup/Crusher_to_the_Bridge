## Design decisions

- Namespace: `campaigns/contam/phase2_cruise_contrasts_01/` (manifest §6), submitted only through `scripts/campaign`.
- Option A field pre-stage (no ContamX in any image): `cell.py --mode field-cache` solves all 32 fields locally (28 commissioned E exports — 7 arms × 4 hulls — plus 4 committed-PRJ P baselines) with the official NIST ContamX `3.4.0.3 64 bit` binary, verified against the frozen SHA256 `d14dc8020bd5e9f95962af52f46b7ce85e2899b63306309d6590cec652274b2b` (tarball `3612798dff9a4f1ea4f0279c4c7b526b244b88875dea425fafd868f3570df4fa`, `https://www.nist.gov/document/contam-x-3403-linux-64bittargz`, U.S. public domain). The cache is keyed `<hull>:<lane>:<arm>` and every field records the ops, `edits`, filter efficiency at export, realized per-group `ach`/`hvac_duty`/`oa_fraction`, a `field_sha256`, and the solve wall-clock; trajectory cells refuse a cached field whose recorded dependencies differ from the arm (stale-field guard). Upload target: `s3://crusherbucket-994254241749-us-east-1-an/campaign/contam_phase2_cruise_contrasts_01/field_cache/field_cache.json`. `--phase1-field-cache` performs the report-only byte comparison of the two `expedition_cruise_450` baseline fields with the Phase 1 cache (SHA256 `b91665323192f95591f538aeb9badfefc1c6e7393aa7c4cafaef0748a496f5b5`).
- Arm application: `cell.py` applies the arm's ops through `tools.contam_fleet_compare._apply_arm_ops` on a copy of the platform `air_flow_paths.json`; N builds the native engine from the mutated JSON with the arm's filter efficiency; E/P build `ContamXTransportEngine` from the cached field with the arm's filter efficiency and OA. Every cell artifact carries an `arm_record` (ops, edits, engine args, realized per-group settings with changed/unchanged counts, realized supply gains, DC04 `r_low` feasibility). The DC04 arm fails closed if any supply group's `r_low` leaves `[0, 1]`. P rejects any arm other than `baseline`.
- Output contract: Phase 1 `cell_{seed}.json` schema family (`metrics` incl. `zone_stats`, `path_flow_summary`, `field_audit`, `field_source`, `timing`, `engine`) plus `arm`, `arm_record`, `ahs_flow_table` (realized per-AHS flows for DC05) and `field_sha256`. Schema id `contam_phase2_cruise_contrasts_cell_v1`.
- Readout: `readout.py` checks coverage against `campaign.json`, aggregates per hull × lane × arm, forms paired N − E per arm and P − N baseline, arm − baseline, DC01 ordering pass/violation lists (B_V and per-zone E_i), DC02 O − G with `max_i |ΔE_i|`, DC04 A − R / A − B / R − B with reductions, gap, per-zone ΔE_i sign-flip zones and Edison's 5-pp tie band (report-only), DC05 arm-effect parity and realized AHS flows, DC07 source-class × receptor tables (local/remote, p50/p95/max, time-to-peak, first arrival, never-reached), and the pilot gate with the extrapolation bounds.
- Image: `campaign.json` carries the bare tag `contam-phase2-cruise-contrasts-01`; the pilot session must build the image from a `main` containing this campaign and pin the digest in `campaign.json` / `design/build.py` before the first submit (`image_digest_pinned: false`, `pin_image_digest_before_submit: true` in the design), exactly as the Phase 1 pilot PR did.
- No `engines/` or `tools/` change; no epidemiological constant; `campaigns/contam/phase1_hvac_tracer_01/` untouched.
