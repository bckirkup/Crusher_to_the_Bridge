# CONTAM Phase 1 HVAC tracer readout

**Status:** Measured transport-correctness / source-realization panel readout. No epidemiology claims.

Machine-readable output: [`readout/phase1_readout.json`](readout/phase1_readout.json), generated by `campaigns/contam/phase1_hvac_tracer_01/readout.py` from the S3 campaign prefix and pre-staged field cache.

## Scope and lane honesty

- Phase 1 is a tracer-only HVAC field-generation panel: 1e6 tracer units, `natural_decay_rate=0`, `filter_efficiency=0.5`, 12 one-hour epochs, seeds 1-20.
- Lanes N, E, and P share the `engines/py_contam_bridge.py` matrix-exponential mass propagator; N/E/P differences isolate airflow-field generation, not independent contaminant-solver validation.
- P is a bounded committed-PRJ baseline for the five hulls with committed Contam bundles; `fletcher_class_destroyer` is N/E only because it has no committed bundle.
- No agents, pathogens, dose-response, epidemiological constants, VSP/Park/attack-rate anchors, Ship X, S lane, or Edison data-return packaging are part of this readout.
